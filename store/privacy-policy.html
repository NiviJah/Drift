Drift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

Drift: Sleep Sounds & White Noise ("Drift", "the app") does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works entirely offline.

Information stored on your device

Your saved mixes, favourite sounds, sleep-timer preferences and settings are stored locally on your device so the app can remember them between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and you can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset all data.

Permissions

  • Background audio — keeps the sounds you chose playing while your screen is locked.
  • Notifications (Android) — shows playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback when you tap controls.

Drift never accesses your microphone, camera, photos, location or contacts. On iOS a microphone description is present only because the audio engine library supports recording; the permission is never requested.

Children's privacy

Drift is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
