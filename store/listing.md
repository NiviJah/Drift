## Full description

Drift helps you fall asleep, stay focused and tune out the world with beautifully crafted ambient sound.

**Build your perfect mix**
Layer up to eight sounds at once and set the volume of each one individually. Rain on a tent with a crackling campfire and far-off thunder? Brown noise under a ticking clock? It's your mix.

**35 high-quality sounds**
• Noise: white, pink, brown, grey, green and a deep rumble
• Rain: light rain, heavy rain, rain on a tent, thunder
• Water: ocean waves, forest stream, waterfall, underwater
• Nature: campfire, birdsong, crickets, night pond, cat purr
• Air: wind, box fan, air conditioner, airplane cabin, hair dryer
• Home: shushing, heartbeat, ticking clock, night train, clothes dryer
• Tones: delta, theta, alpha and beta binaural beats, Om drone, singing bowls

Every loop is seamless — no gaps, no clicks — and living sounds like thunder and birdsong are randomised so they never repeat the same way twice.

**Find sounds fast**
Search by name or mood ("baby", "focus", "storm"), filter by category, and keep your favourites one tap away.

**Save your mixes**
Save, rename and update your own mixes, or start from curated collections for Sleep, Focus, Relax and Baby.

**A sleep timer that fades gently**
Choose how long to play and how long to fade. Volume lowers little by little over the last minutes — or across the whole timer — so the silence never wakes you.

**Made for the night**
• Keeps playing with the screen locked and from the lock screen / notification controls
• Pauses for calls and resumes afterwards
• Pauses when headphones are unplugged (optional)
• Gentle fade-in when you press play
• Dim bedside clock mode
• Optionally play alongside podcasts or music

**Private by design**
No account, no ads, no tracking. Drift works completely offline and nothing ever leaves your phone.

## Apple review notes

> Drift is an ambient-sound mixer. To test: tap any two sounds on the Sounds tab — playback starts immediately. Open the Timer tab to try the fading sleep timer. Background audio (UIBackgroundModes: audio) is used to keep the user's chosen sounds playing while the screen is locked; playback is always started by the user. The app never records audio; NSMicrophoneUsageDescription is present only because the bundled audio engine library links recording APIs, and the permission is never requested. No login is required.
