**Fixes playback over Bluetooth and car audio.**

- Fixed: pressing play while connected to a car or Bluetooth device flickered and returned to paused
  - Drift now requests audio focus every time playback starts
  - Pause commands and focus changes that cars send the moment playback starts are ignored
  - If the audio output can't start, the audio engine rebuilds itself and retries once
- New: *Settings → Playback diagnostics* — a shareable log of what the app and your phone did, to help track down device-specific audio problems
